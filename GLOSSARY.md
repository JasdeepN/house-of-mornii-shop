# House of Mornii

A storefront for regal costume jewellery, backed by Shopify as the system of record for catalog, carts, and customers.

## Language

**Product**:
A single jewellery item offered for sale, with one or more Variants.
_Avoid_: Piece, item, jewelry

**Variant**:
A purchasable option combination of a Product (for example size or colour) with its own price and availability.
_Avoid_: SKU, option

**Collection**:
A named, curated group of Products.
_Avoid_: Category, line, range

**Cart**:
The set of Variants a shopper intends to buy, kept until checkout.
_Avoid_: Basket, bag

**Cart line**:
One Variant and its quantity within a Cart.
_Avoid_: Cart item, merchandise

**Customer**:
A person who signs in to view their orders, addresses, and details.
_Avoid_: Account, user, buyer

**Order**:
A completed purchase belonging to a Customer.
_Avoid_: Purchase, transaction
