# House of Mornii

A storefront for regal costume jewellery, backed by Shopify as the system of record for catalog, carts, and customers.

## Language

**Product**:
A single jewellery item offered for sale, with one or more Variants.
_Avoid_: Piece, item, jewelry

**Variant**:
A purchasable option combination of a Product (for example size or colour) with its own price and availability.
_Avoid_: SKU, option

**Collection**:
A named, curated group of Products.
_Avoid_: Category, line, range

**Cart**:
The set of Variants a Shopper intends to buy in one browser, kept until checkout. Shoppers see it called their **Bag**; everywhere else it is the Cart.
_Avoid_: Basket; "bag" anywhere but the words Shoppers read

**Cart line**:
One Variant and its quantity within a Cart.
_Avoid_: Cart item, merchandise

**Shopper**:
Anyone using the storefront, whether or not they are signed in.
_Avoid_: visitor, anon user, guest, user

**Customer**:
A person the store knows by email, who can sign in to see their Orders, addresses, and details.
_Avoid_: Account, user, buyer

**Customer session**:
The period during which a Customer is signed in within one browser, from signing in until signing out or expiry.
_Avoid_: login, log in, log out, auth, account session

**Order**:
A completed purchase belonging to a Customer.
_Avoid_: Purchase, transaction
